Project Abstract

About This System

Examination Seating Arrangement System

The Examination Seating Arrangement System is designed to automate and optimize the process of allocating seats for students during examinations across multiple departments — CSE, ECE, AIDS, and CSIT — at Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation. The main objective of the system is to create a fair, organized, and conflict-free seating arrangement while considering various constraints such as adjacency conflicts, room capacity, and special student requirements.

The system intelligently assigns seats in a way that prevents students from sitting next to each other when conflicts exist, thereby reducing malpractice opportunities and improving examination discipline. Department-wise distribution ensures that students from different branches are interleaved — CSE, ECE, AIDS, and CSIT students are mixed systematically within each hall to maximize examination fairness.

In addition, the system is capable of handling special accommodations for students with specific needs and can detect over-constrained scenarios where seating allocation becomes impossible under the given conditions. In such cases, the system provides clear explanations for the failure, helping administrators identify and resolve the issue efficiently.

The project features a complete admin dashboard with student management, hall ticket verification, real-time updates, analytics, attendance tracking, notification system, and PDF export capabilities. The project enhances accuracy, reduces manual effort, saves time, and ensures better management of examination halls. Overall, this system provides a smart and reliable solution for modern examination seating management.

How It Works

Input Data

3

Auto Mix & Assign

Algorithm rotates CSE→ECE→AIDS→CSIT pattern ensuring perfect inter-department mixing.

4

Verify & Confirm

Hall ticket verification and conflict scan runs before finalizing the arrangement.

5

Export & Publish

Download PDF charts, print attendance sheets, and notify students via the announcement system.
